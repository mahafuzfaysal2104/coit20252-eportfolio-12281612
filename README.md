## About this e-portfolio

This repository is my individual e-portfolio for COIT20252, developed across
the term to demonstrate my understanding and application of Business Process
Management (BPM) concepts. It brings together selected artefacts — videos,
articles, websites, and my own tutorial work — alongside reflective
justifications explaining what each artefact taught me and how it connects
to core BPM ideas covered in lectures and tutorials.

The portfolio is organised around three key topics, each developed and
submitted at a different point in the term.
